# Register an account

Create a My Team account before setting up your squad.

**Flow:** Home page → Register → Enter your details → Sign up → Overview → Set up a squad

1. Open My Team and select **Register**.
2. Enter your **First name**, **Last name**, **Email address**, and **Password**.
3. Select **Sign up**. My Team creates your account and signs you in.
4. Continue to [set up a squad]({{ '/en/set-up-a-squad.html' | relative_url }}).
