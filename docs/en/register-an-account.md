# Register an account

Create a My Team account before setting up your squad.

**Flow:** Logged-out home page → Register → Enter your details → Sign up → Verify your email → Sign in → Set up a squad

1. Open My Team. When you are signed out, the home page offers **Login** and **Register**.

   ![My Team home page while signed out, with the Register button](../assets/en/setup-registration-home.png)

2. Select **Register** to open the account form.

   ![Create your account form in My Team](../assets/en/setup-registration-form.png)

3. Enter your **First name**, **Last name**, **Email address**, and **Password**, then select **Sign up**. My Team creates your account and sends a verification email. Registration does not sign you in.

   ![Confirmation that a verification email was sent](../assets/en/setup-registration.png)

4. Open the verification email and select its link. It opens `/verify-email?token=…`, where My Team checks the token. The link expires after 24 hours.

   ![Email verification success with a link to sign in](../assets/en/setup-registration-verified.png)
5. When the page confirms your email address, select **Sign in** and log in with the email address and password you registered with.
6. Continue to [set up a squad]({{ '/en/set-up-a-squad.html' | relative_url }}).
