# Trainer

A Trainer is a Squad-scoped Person with the `trainer` role. Trainers may log in,
run Spond synchronization, and manage activity planning.

Trainers share activity-planning permissions with Admins: they may manage local
Invitations where applicable, selection, Teams, lineups, formations, shirts, and
attendance. They may not manage Squad membership/configuration or link or
unlink a Spond group.

A Trainer may be the single responsible person for a Team. A Guardian may also
serve as a Team's responsible person where the planning workflow requires it.
