# Shirt Set

A Shirt Set is reusable kit inventory containing Shirts with a sponsor, color,
number, size, goalkeeper flag, and availability status.

## Rules

- Shirt numbers are unique within a Shirt Set.
- A Team may reference one Shirt Set.
- Players in that Team may use only Shirts from the assigned Shirt Set.
- Each Player may have at most one Shirt in a Team.
- A specific Shirt may be assigned to at most one Team in the same activity.
- Multiple Teams in one activity may use the same Shirt Set.
- A Shirt Set cannot be deleted while referenced by any Team.

Shirt Sets are reusable across activities and Teams. Shirt assignments are
planning data owned by this application, including for Spond-linked Squads.
